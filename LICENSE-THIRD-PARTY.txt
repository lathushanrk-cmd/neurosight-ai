NEUROSIGHT AI is an original experimental prototype assembled for local research demonstration.

External software loaded via npm during setup:
- React and React DOM: MIT
- Vite: MIT
- TypeScript: Apache-2.0
- MediaPipe Tasks Vision: Apache-2.0 (see upstream for model-specific notes)
- Recharts: MIT
- Lucide React: ISC

MediaPipe face_landmarker.task is fetched from the official Google AI Edge model endpoint (or a verified mirror) during installation, subject to Google's licensing and applicable model terms. Its exact SHA-256 is checked before use.
The original license texts for these dependencies are installed via npm inside node_modules.
