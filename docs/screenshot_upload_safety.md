# Authentic screenshot provenance and privacy

Eight screenshots of the locally running NEUROSIGHT AI application are now included in `docs/images/` and linked from the README.

The screenshots show the actual application interface and its displayed measurements; they are **not AI-recreated result graphs**.

## Privacy handling

The source webcam screenshot contained a person. The version committed as `docs/images/02-dashboard-live-private.png` is **privacy-redacted**: the camera scene, face and body have been blurred to reduce identifying detail; the eye-area landmark overlays and original in-app badges remain visible. This does not guarantee anonymity, particularly where eye-region features remain visible. The original unredacted webcam screenshot has **not** been intentionally committed in the published files. Avoid publishing the original capture or other identifiable camera frames.

## Scientific interpretation

The displayed prototype sessions include usable iris landmarks and brightness traces but lack reliably measurable pupil boundaries. The application therefore correctly marks pupil anomaly assessment **unavailable**. The screenshots demonstrate interface functions rather than validating pupil accuracy or any neurological diagnostic claim.

For a public repository, do not add any unredacted camera captures, recordings, participant data or other identifying material without appropriate permission.
